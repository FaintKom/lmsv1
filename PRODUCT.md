# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Students** of schools and online courses. They open GrassLMS to do the
  next lesson or exercise, often on a phone, and come back daily for streaks
  and XP.
- **Teachers** run live lessons, set homework, mark work and keep the
  gradebook, attendance and journal.
- **Methodists** build courses and lessons in the block editor and author
  exercises.
- **School admins** manage users, groups, schedules and billing; a super admin
  runs the platform.
- **Parents** get a read-only view of their child's progress.

Primary market: schools and course creators in the EU and North America.
English is the primary locale; the interface ships in six languages (English,
Spanish, Russian, Turkish, German, Ukrainian) and every screen has to look the
same in all of them, Cyrillic included.

## Product Purpose

A SaaS learning platform for the three subjects where a generic LMS gives up:
programming needs a place to run code, mathematics needs the answer checked
rather than the keystrokes matched, and languages need drills beyond multiple
choice. Success means a school can run its whole teaching week in GrassLMS and
a student does the next task without being told where to click.

## Positioning

GrassLMS grades all three subjects on the server: 27 exercise types, student
code in an isolated sandbox across five languages, and correct answers that
never reach the student's browser. It also runs a lesson while it is
happening: the whole class on one task, a shared board, and a live panel of
who is stuck.

## Constraints

- Brand name is **GrassLMS**, one word. Domain `grasslms.online`.
- Licence is proprietary (All Rights Reserved). Never describe the product as
  open-source.
- No paid checkout yet; card payments are not live.
- No invented numbers, testimonials or customer names anywhere. Every figure
  on a public page must be countable in the repository.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes; 44×44 touch targets; phone
  layouts pass the checks from specs/065–067.
- Fonts are self-hosted with open licences; no runtime loading from Google
  Fonts.
- Student-side game elements (voxel avatar, student room, XP, streaks) keep
  their own look; the owner decided to leave them as they are (2026-09-30).
- Lesson content (theory HTML, JSXGraph widgets, KaTeX) belongs to the course
  author; the interface frames it and does not restyle it.

## Voice

Plain, specific, and calm. Say what happened and what to do next. No
"unlock", "discover", "seamless" or similar marketing filler.
